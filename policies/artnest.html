Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

Welcome to ArtNest: Kids Color And Fun. Your privacy and safety are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information within our application.

1. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

ArtNest is a family-friendly creative app suitable for users of all ages, including young children. In accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws that protect children, we do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13. No account creation or personal profile is required to use the app.

2. Information Collection and Use

  • Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location data.
  • No Advertising: ArtNest contains no ads and no in-app purchases. We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not track users across other apps or websites.
  • Device Permissions: The app may request access to your device's Gallery/Photos. This permission is used only to allow the user to save their completed artwork locally on the device. We do not access your private photos, and no images are transmitted to our servers or shared with any third parties.
  • Notifications: The app may request permission to send notifications. These are used only to send friendly reminders encouraging the user to return and continue their creative activity. No personal data is collected through notifications, and this permission can be revoked at any time from your device settings.
  • Technical Information: To keep the app stable and working well, we automatically receive limited, non-personal technical information such as crash reports, device model, operating system version, app version, and basic usage statistics (for example, which activity of the app was opened). This information is not used to identify any individual child and is never used for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

ArtNest uses the following third-party service, which may process the non-personal technical information described above on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase: We use Firebase services (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, and Cloud Storage) to understand app stability, fix problems, deliver app updates and new coloring content, and improve the experience. Firebase may use a randomly generated app-instance identifier for these purposes. Learn more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Parental Gate

To ensure safety, external links (such as "Rate Us" or "Privacy Policy") are protected by a Parental Gate (for example, a simple math problem) to prevent accidental clicks by children.

5. Data Security & Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no risk of your personal identity being compromised through the app. The limited technical information described above is retained only as long as necessary to maintain and improve the app, in line with the retention periods of the service providers. No personal data is stored on our servers.

6. Data Deletion

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no personal user data to delete. Any locally saved artwork and coloring progress is stored only on the user's device and can be deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. If you have any question about data related to your device, you can contact us at the email below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
